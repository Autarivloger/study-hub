# Cybersecurity Week 8 — Connect the foundations and review

Seven English lessons and44 reasoned checks. Six main days have four checks each;
Day7 has20 closed-notes foundation questions. All essential explanations and
case instructions are embedded in study-hub.html. Existing notes, navigation,
answer reveal and storage remain unchanged.

## Study sequence

1. Foundations/request trace: valued assets, possible harm, relevant weaknesses,
   controls, device/network/identity/app/storage boundaries and model limitations.
2. Personal review/function map: current account/update/permission/recovery
   evidence, match/mismatch/unknown and interacting Govern/Identify/Protect/
   Detect/Respond/Recover outcomes.
3. Connected logs: reuse Week7 raw records, compare units/cutoffs, retain unknown
   time, distinguish source claims, hypotheses and next discriminating evidence.
4. Lab assessment: permitted scope/methods, evidence plan, model-versus-real
   results, findings, confidence, accountable actions and closure criteria.
5. Recovery/portfolio: response roles/trade-offs, raw protection, RPO/RTO,
   tested-versus-planned recovery and a sanitised evidence-backed portfolio.
6. Readiness: changed examples, six core tasks, targeted revision, next-stage
   goal and plain-English automation boundaries/failure tests.
7. Foundation capstone: complete workbook/portfolio,20 cross-week checks,
   consistency review and strengths/gaps before choosing further learning.

The roadmap stays at eight weeks/seven days each:56 prepared study days.
Further weeks are not silently added, and Day8.7's existing Next day remains
disabled. Completing material is not presented as expert qualification.

## Important claim boundaries

The six CSF2.0 functions organise interacting outcomes; they are not a mandatory
linear incident sequence, exhaustive checklist or course certification.
Model decisions are not real server/authentication/SQL verification. Prepared
application logs are source claims, not supplied independent state snapshots.
Download/installation/active-update verification differ. Planned recovery is
not performed recovery. Byte agreement is not event truth or usable restoration.
Private, public, unlisted and delivered-to-visitors data have different readers.
Unknown observations, timezone and applicability remain explicit.
